Support · Privacy policy

RunSiege support

RunSiege records outdoor activities and turns the routes you cover into territory on the game map.

GPS recording

Start an outdoor activity and allow location access when prompted. You can change this permission in iPhone Settings.

Apple Watch import

Connect Apple Health in the app to import compatible outdoor workouts with a saved GPS route. You can manage this access in the Health app.

Teams and account

Shared map contributions require iCloud on your device. In Profile you can manage sharing and request deletion of your app account and associated data.

Get help

Open a support issue with your app version, iOS version and the steps to reproduce the problem. Issues are public, so leave out account details, health information and GPS routes.

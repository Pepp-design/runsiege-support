RunSiege privacy policy

RunSiege, by Giuseppe Giametta, uses data to record outdoor activity and operate its territory game.

Activity and location

When you record an activity, RunSiege uses your location to measure distance, draw the route and calculate captured areas. Full GPS routes and activity history stay on your device and may be included in device backups according to your Apple backup settings.

Apple Health

With your permission, RunSiege reads the type, date, duration and GPS route of compatible outdoor walking, running and hiking workouts from Apple Watch. It does not read heart rate or calories. The full route stays on the device. You can separately opt in to sharing results derived from these workouts with your team.

iCloud and shared game data

The app uses CloudKit for teams, player identifiers, player names and avatars, activity totals and captures. Shared captures record the player name, map area and capture time. The game grid is approximately 20 metres per side. These records are stored in a public CloudKit database and may reveal places players visit. Sharing results from Apple Watch can include distance, activity totals and captured areas. If you link your Apple Account, the app uses an account identifier and an iCloud Keychain backup of your profile and team to help restore them.

Your choices

You can manage location access in iPhone Settings and Apple Health access in the Health app. You can also change team sharing in Profile. Switching off Apple Watch sharing stops new uploads from imported workouts and requests removal of identifiable Apple Watch captures. In Profile, use the account deletion option to remove local and associated synced data. Your Apple Account is managed separately by Apple.

Tracking and contact

RunSiege does not use this data for advertising or tracking across apps and websites. For questions about privacy or help exercising your rights, use the support page. Do not include private data in a public issue.
